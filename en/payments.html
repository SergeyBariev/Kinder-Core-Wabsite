Clear pricing

Use your CRM without a monthly subscription

KinderCore does not charge organisations a monthly fee for using the CRM. Our remuneration is linked to the organisation's payments.

KinderCore remuneration

0.3%of SBP payments
1.0%of card payments

There is no monthly subscription fee for KinderCore. Our remuneration is calculated from payments made to your organisation: 0.3% for SBP and 1.0% for card payments.

Your organisation remains the recipient of class fees. KinderCore does not receive or hold parents' money. Bank acquiring and processing fees are paid separately by the organisation under its agreement with the bank.

KinderCore remuneration is governed by a separate business-to-business arrangement with the organisation. Payment setup depends on the organisation's agreement with its bank or payment provider.

What to remember

  • No monthly CRM subscription fee.
  • KinderCore remuneration is linked to the organisation's payments.
  • The organisation receives the class fees.
  • Bank fees are not KinderCore revenue.
