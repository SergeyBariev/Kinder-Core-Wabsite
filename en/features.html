KinderCore features

The key workflows in one CRM

KinderCore brings together clients, groups, classes, subscriptions, staff and finances.

01

Children, parents and families

Maintain child and parent profiles, connect multiple representatives to a child and keep family relationships organised.

02

Programmes, groups and schedules

Organise programmes and groups, assign teachers, manage class schedules, changes and exceptions.

03

Attendance register

Record attendance and absences, keep visit history and track related class deductions.

04

Subscriptions and classes

Manage subscriptions, class counts, prices, dates and statuses, with related attendance operations.

05

Staff and access roles

Manage team members, invitations, roles, positions and organisation-level access.

06

Payments and financial reports

Record payments and refunds and review receipts, discounts, accruals and management reports.

07

Organisation settings

Manage organisation settings and legal details. A user can belong to multiple organisations with separate roles.

08

Management workspace

Use overview sections and reports to keep track of operations, financial indicators and team activity.
