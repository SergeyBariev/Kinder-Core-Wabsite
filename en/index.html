A clear pricing model

CRM without a monthly subscription

You do not pay a monthly subscription fee for KinderCore. Our remuneration is linked to payments processed for your organisation through connected payment methods.

KinderCore remuneration: 0.3% of payments via SBP and 1.0% of card payments. Your organisation remains the recipient of class fees — KinderCore does not receive or hold parents' money.
