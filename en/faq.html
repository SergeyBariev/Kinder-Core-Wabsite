FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about KinderCore, pricing and everyday use.

Who is KinderCore for?

Private childcare and Montessori centres, learning centres, clubs, sports sections, creative studios, language schools and organisations running classes for children or adults.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. There is no monthly subscription fee for KinderCore. Remuneration is 0.3% of SBP payments and 1.0% of card payments.

Who receives payments from parents?

The organisation remains the recipient of payments for its services. KinderCore does not receive or hold parents' money. Bank fees are paid separately under the organisation's bank agreement.

Can I manage multiple groups and programmes?

Yes. KinderCore includes programmes, groups, schedules, teacher assignment and attendance tracking.

Can I manage staff access?

Yes. The organisation can manage staff invitations, roles, positions and access.

Is there a parent mobile app?

The main KinderCore interface is an administrative CRM for organisations. A parent portal and mobile app are not part of the current core scope.
